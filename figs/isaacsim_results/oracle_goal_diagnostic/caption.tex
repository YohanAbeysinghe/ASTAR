\textbf{Isaac Sim evaluation with oracle goals.}
GEAR-Nav is evaluated over 50 pedestrian timing variants in each of three
fixed scenes (150 metric trials). Third-person images show separate
collision-free success reruns and are excluded from the metric denominator.
The middle row plots all recorded robot trajectories in a fixed local frame
for each scene, with a shared metric scale. Bold orange highlights one metric
trial; pale orange shows the remaining trials. Red identifies the trial with
physical contact; its cross marks the last recorded pose, not the location of
contact. Stars and shaded disks denote the goal and its 1.5\,m success radius.
The lower row reports success and actual-contact counts with nominal 95\%
Wilson intervals; each dot represents one metric trial. Trials vary
pedestrian timing at fixed geometry, and intervals are conditional on this
protocol. The policy receives exact simulator goal coordinates transformed
into its local frame, so these results are an oracle-goal diagnostic rather
than a language-only evaluation. They do not establish statistical
significance against another method.

\textbf{Isaac Sim navigation evaluation.}
Success rate, collision rate, and obstacle clearance are shown for GEAR-Nav,
OmniVLA, TIC-VLA, and NaVILA across three scenarios. Higher success and
clearance and lower collision rate are preferred. GEAR-Nav is highlighted in
orange, with the same method colors used in the real-world figure. Baselines
are reproduced from VEGA; GEAR-Nav values follow the updated simulation table.
Rates are displayed as percentages and clearance in meters. The upper images
are illustrative third-person views from separate recorded runs; they are not
matched to the aggregate results below. Bars show the supplied aggregate
values, without inferred trial counts or uncertainty estimates.
